Welcome to the official GitHub profile of NaYa Fintech! We are a consulting firm specializing in the financial services sector, leveraging technology to solve complex business challenges. Our primary focus is on developing robust Python scripts that formulate trading strategies, backtest them using historical market data, and perform comprehensive risk analysis.

## 💡 What We Do
- Design and implement trading strategies using quantitative techniques.
- Backtest strategies with large-scale historical datasets sourced from the internet.
- Develop and automate risk analysis frameworks to assess portfolio and strategy performance.
- Automate data extraction, transformation, and analysis to streamline financial research workflows.
- Bridge the gap between business requirements and technical solutions in fast-paced financial environments.

## 🛠️ Key Skills & Tools
- **Python**: pandas, numpy, scipy, cvxopt
- Data Analysis & Statistical Modeling
- Backtesting & Strategy Evaluation
- Risk Analysis & Portfolio Optimization
- Financial Data Engineering

## 🚀 Featured Projects
